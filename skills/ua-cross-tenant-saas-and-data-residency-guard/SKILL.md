# UA Cross-Tenant SaaS & Data Residency Guard (Oblast)

## Summary

**Refuse vs localize vs conditional** is a risk budget, not a brand preference: **REFUSE** when data includes **special-category** citizen payloads, **classified** or **operational-security** content, **irreversible** third-party **model-training** defaults you cannot contractually disable, or **subprocessor opacity** that breaks **ua_personal_data_and_official_stats_governance_auditor** RoPA/TIA discipline. **LOCALIZE** (self-host or **contractual EU/EEA region pinning** with **key custody** in your control plane) for **hosted Grafana / Prometheus remote_write**, **Metabase Cloud**, **managed OpenSearch**, **LLM inference** where prompts may contain **PII or crisis comms**, and **session recordings** of staff SSO—mirror **ring-global-data-residency-dpa-and-subprocessors-specialist** “runtime property” rule: marketing **region badges** must match **packet paths** and **backup regions**. **CONDITIONAL** only with: signed **DPA / processing agreement** (controller↔processor) **and** counsel-approved **transfer tool** for any non-UA storage; **subprocessor register** subscription + **30-day objection workflow** where EU-style terms apply; **DPF / SCC / UK IDTA** artefacts filed beside the architecture diagram; **geopolitical_risk_analyst** sign-off when vendor group is **US-listed**, **RU/BY touch risk**, or **Middle-East / CN** hosting that triggers **sanctions** or **data access** concerns. For **LLM APIs** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, Vertex): treat **retention**, **abuse monitoring**, **fine-tuning**, and **evaluation** flags as **four separate toggles**—default **no training on customer data** must be **screenshotted + URL version** in the annex. **US cloud regions** (e.g. `us-east-1`) are not “unsafe by magic” but **raise** **CLOUD Act** and **subprocessor** jurisdiction questions—document **legal entity** (e.g. **Ireland Ltd** vs **US Inc.**) and **support access** stories. **Hosted Grafana / Datadog / Honeycomb**: classify whether **query payloads** can contain **citizen narrative** from 311 tickets—if yes, **same tier as LLM**. **Cross-border model training** for **internal** oblast models: separate **training data residency** from **inference residency**; forbid mixing **citizen PII** into vendor **foundation-model** fine-tunes without explicit lawful basis and **DPIA** path. **UA procurement**: track **registered cloud / DC provider** requirements—plan **Helm-on-UA/EU** alternatives before deadlines. **Anti-pattern**: signing **clickwrap** SaaS with oblast procurement card without **subprocessor** diff review; **anti-pattern**: piping **Diia** or **Monobank**-sourced attributes into **US analytics** “just for dashboards”.

## Instructions

1. Pattern: REFUSE_LOCALIZE_CONDITIONAL_GATE — score {data_sensitivity, legal_uncertainty, geopolitical_risk, vendor_lock_in}; two dimensions **high** ⇒ default REFUSE or LOCALIZE.
2. Pattern: DPA_ANNEX_FOUR_PACK — purposes + categories + retention + subprocessors URL + transfer_mechanism (counsel-filled) + LLM_training_flags_versioned.
3. Pattern: SUBPROCESSOR_DIFF_REVIEW — vendor email + JSON diff of subprocessor list stored in Plane issue; Mattermost alert on net-new countries.
4. Pattern: GEO_CROSSCHECK_MIN — geopolitical_risk_analyst answers: HQ domicile, listing/exposure, sanctions proximity, wartime continuity, secondary jurisdiction for disputes.
5. Pattern: PACKET_PROOF_NOT_SLA — traceroute / egress allowlist / billing region line ≠ legal residency; only **contract + telemetry** together count.
